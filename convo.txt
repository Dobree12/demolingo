Great, clean workspace. Here's my refined proposal with the psychology baked in:

🧠 The 5 Psychological Techniques I'll Build Into the App
Based on research, these are the most powerful and relevant techniques for your use case:

1. 🔄 Repetiție Spațiată (Spaced Repetition)
Words you struggle with come back more often. Words you know well appear less. The app remembers your brain better than you do. This fights the "forgetting curve" — the #1 enemy of language learning.

2. 🎰 Recompense Variabile (Variable Reward Schedules)
Not every correct answer gets the same reaction. Sometimes it's a simple "Bravo!", sometimes it's confetti + bonus XP + a surprise badge. The unpredictability creates a dopamine loop that makes you want to keep going — like "one more lesson" turns into five.

3. 🪜 Victorii Mici (Small Wins / Chunking)
Every lesson is broken into bite-sized pieces (5-8 exercises). Each completed step triggers a mini celebration. This creates momentum — "I finished one, might as well do the next." The progress bar fills visually with satisfying animations.

4. 🌱 Mentalitatea de Creștere (Growth Mindset)
When you get something wrong, the app NEVER says "Greșit!" harshly. Instead:

"Aproape! Hai să încercăm din nou 💪" (Almost! Let's try again)
"Nu ai nimerit acum, dar ești pe drumul cel bun!" (You missed it now, but you're on the right track!)
"Greșelile sunt cele mai bune profesoare 📚" (Mistakes are the best teachers)
5. 🏆 Autonomie + Competență (Self-Determination Theory)
The user CHOOSES their daily goal, CHOOSES which lesson to tackle, and SEES clear proof of their competence growing (words mastered counter, accuracy %, fluency level). Feeling in control = staying motivated.
